# Getting Started with SEO in 2025

SEO in 2025 is about creating helpful, structured content that both humans and AI search engines can understand and cite directly.

## What is SEO

Search Engine Optimization is the practice of improving your website so it appears higher in search results when people look for topics related to your business.

## Why it matters in 2025

Over 90% of online experiences start with a search engine. If your website does not appear on page 1, most potential customers never find you.

## Three things to fix first

Every page on your site needs a proper title tag, a meta description, and at least one H1 heading. These are the basics that most websites still get wrong.

## How AI search engines are changing SEO

Tools like Perplexity and ChatGPT now answer questions by citing web pages directly. To be cited, your content needs to answer questions clearly in the first paragraph.

## Next steps

Start by auditing your existing pages, fixing missing metadata, and publishing one helpful piece of content per week targeting a keyword your competitors rank for.
